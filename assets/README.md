# Ferrie brand assets

`ferrie-otter-line.png` is the approved logo: a full-body otter in side profile, drawn with simple blue contours and carrying a small app installation tile. This version uses a white background, not transparency. Both READMEs now use the matching `ferrie-banner-zh.png` and `ferrie-banner-en.png` banners (2172 × 724).

Earlier concepts are retained as `ferrie-otter.png`, `ferrie-logo.png`, and `ferrie-logo-install.png`.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The final image is saved without post-processing. Preserve its aspect ratio and clear space.

## Design prompt

```text
Redesign the attached Ferrie otter mascot according to this new direction: SIDE PROFILE, FULL BODY, SIMPLE LINE ART.
Retain only the species and gentle capable character from the reference. Entirely replace the front-facing colored cartoon portrait.
A graceful otter in strict side profile facing right, sitting naturally on its haunches, with a long tapered tail sweeping along the ground behind it to the left. Show the ENTIRE animal including head, torso, forepaws, hind foot and tail. Small rounded ear, short recognizable otter muzzle, one tiny dot eye. Natural elegant proportions, small head relative to body, not chibi, not a teddy bear.
One forepaw gently carries a small rounded-square app tile against the chest; the tile has a tiny simple downward arrow. The tile is a subtle small accent, not the main silhouette. Keep the animal dominant.
Minimal contemporary monoline logo: a few beautifully flowing continuous curves, consistent medium-weight BLUE stroke with round caps and round joins. OPEN UNFILLED interiors, genuinely transparent inside and outside outlines. No colored body fills, no white body fill. Only the tiny eye and nose may be filled dots. Extremely restrained interior detail: one foreleg curve and one hind-leg curve. No individual fingers, no fur strokes, no whiskers, no facial shading, no big eyes, no highlights, no ornamental details.
Elegant economical outline drawing suitable for a refined open-source tool identity. Strong distinctive otter silhouette, clear at small size, generous negative space. Balanced horizontal composition on a square canvas with comfortable margins, no cropping. One single blue ink color, transparent alpha background. No text, no wordmark, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no scenery, no water lines, no borders. The new side-view request overrides the earlier front-view symmetry.
```

## Final background correction prompt

```text
Keep this exact side-profile FULL BODY otter line drawing and small app tile. Produce a clean flat logo on a PURE SOLID WHITE background (#FFFFFF). Replace every gray checkerboard square with pure white, both outside and inside the animal outline. Blue linework must be a solid uniform medium blue, crisp and high contrast. Preserve the small downward arrow inside the app tile, eye, nose, ear, long tail, and elegant thin monoline contour. Preserve the square composition with all of the tail and feet visible and generous whitespace around the animal. No other changes. Absolutely NO glow, NO blur, NO neon, NO gradient, NO shadows, NO texture, NO checkerboard. This should look like a clean blue pen contour drawing printed on plain white paper. Opaque white background intentionally, not transparency.
```
